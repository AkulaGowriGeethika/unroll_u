PRACTISE WITH CARE

Listen to your body.

Yoga should feel controlled and comfortable, not painful. Move within your range, use props or modifications when helpful, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort. If you are pregnant, recovering from an injury or managing a health condition, ask a qualified healthcare professional or yoga teacher about suitable modifications.
